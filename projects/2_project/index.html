F-scheduler: Fast Diffusion ODE Solver

Under Review at TMLR — Training-free 5–8 step ODE sampling outperforming 4-step SOTA distillation models

F-scheduler (Illuminating the Free-Lunch Design Space for Fast Sampling of Diffusion Models) (Li & Bai, 2025) is a training-free time and Free-U skip-connection scheduler designed for few-step (5–8 steps) diffusion ODE solvers (UniPC, DPM++ 2M), currently under review at Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR).

Key Contributions

  • Terminal KL-Aware Time Schedule: Retains a controlled terminal noise level rather than fully denoising to zero variance, enabling the $\beta$-VAE decoder to exploit its KL-regularized latent distribution during few-step generation.
  • Stage-Scheduled Free-U Net Modulation: Identifies why standard Free-U Net degrades few-step (<10) sampling and schedules skip-connection scaling at the optimal inference stage.
  • 5–8 Step ODE Sampling vs. 4-Step Distillation: Compatible with standard ODE solvers (UniPC, DPM++ 2M), generating 1024×1024 images in 6 steps and 512×512 images in 5 steps with FID outperforming 4-step SOTA distillation models (e.g., DMD2 LoRA) and 20-step DPM++ 2M.

  1. TMLR (Sub.)
    F-scheduler: Illuminating the Free-Lunch Design Space for Fast Sampling of Diffusion Models
    Zilai Li and Lujia Bai
    Under Review at Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR), Oct 2025
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML Code Website

    Diffusion models are the state-of-the-art generative models for high-resolution images, but sampling from pretrained models is computationally expensive, motivating interest in fast sampling. Although Free-U Net is a training-free enhancement for improving image quality, we find it ineffective under few-step (<10) sampling. We analyze the discrete diffusion ODE and propose F-scheduler, a scheduler designed for ODE solvers with Free-U Net. Our proposed scheduler consists of a special time schedule that does not fully denoise the feature to enable the use of the KL-term in the beta-VAE decoder, and the schedule of a proper inference stage for modifying the U-Net skip-connection via Free-U Net. Via information theory, we provide insights into how the better scheduled ODE solvers for the diffusion model can outperform the training-based diffusion distillation model. The newly proposed scheduler is compatible with most of the few-step ODE solvers and can sample a 1024x1024-resolution image in 6 steps and a 512x512-resolution image in 5 steps when it applies to DPM++ 2M and UniPC, with an FID result that outperforms the SOTA distillation models and the 20-step DPM++ 2M solver, respectively.
